Rest, breathe, drift

A sleep meditation experience designed to help users settle into rest.

QuietMind guides users through calming audio rituals and gentle wind-down moments before sleep.

QuietMind is a concept for a sleep meditation app focused on reducing nighttime stress and creating a more intentional transition into rest. This site presents the design process, prototypes, and final artifacts behind the product in a format that is easier to browse and understand.
