Sleep meditation app

QuietMind

Overview Design Process Media Team

Effortless relaxation and sleep on time without pills or pressure.

QuietMind guides users through calming audio rituals and gentle wind-down moments before sleep.

QuietMind is a design concept for a sleep meditation app focused on reducing nighttime stress and creating a more intentional transition into rest. This site presents the design process, prototypes, and final documentation created during the design process. QuietMind was developed as a project for CMPSC 185 at UCSB.
